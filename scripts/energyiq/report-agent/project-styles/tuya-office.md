# Tuya Office visual identity

Use a light editorial document, derived from the supplied Tuya Office Energy Report dated 17 August–5 September. This is an appearance reference, not a source of new readings or conclusions.

- White page; ink #1C2530, Tuya orange #FF4E16, secondary steel #1F4E63, muted slate #9AA7B4, warm figure background #F4F3F0 and dividers #E3E1DC.
- Centre an approximately 880px reading column with 28px desktop gutters and generous vertical space. Use system sans-serif; Archivo may be used only if available offline. Body 16px, line-height around 1.55; bold 30–44px title and 22px section headings.
- Compact project/date masthead, dark dividing rule, strong title with restrained orange emphasis. Put decision findings before detailed evidence, following the shared presentation rules.
- Visually numbered evidence sections separated by fine dark rules. Use warm neutral figure panels with understated borders, explanatory captions and readable charts. Orange marks primary energy series or attention; steel and slate distinguish supporting series. Use labelled values as well as colour.
- Tables use horizontal rules, right-aligned tabular numbers and an emphasized total row. Highlight recommendations with a narrow orange rule and a very pale warm background.
- Keep the page a report rather than a dashboard application. Integrate filters or drill-down controls beside their evidence where useful; they do not require a navigation bar, a large dark hero or a grid of equally prominent cards. Two charts may sit together when readable; stack them on narrow screens.
- Adapt titles, charts and analytical questions to current findings. Preserve this visual identity without copying the reference's measured values, dates, chapter count or unsupported maps.

Check at desktop and 390px: conclusions precede evidence, colours and reading width match this style, labels remain readable and the document does not overflow. Use inline assets and working offline controls. Any explicit one-off visual variation stays in that report until an administrator separately activates a new style version.
