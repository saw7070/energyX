# Care-centre portfolio report

Use this method for a monthly or multi-period portfolio of preschools and care centres. Produce a new English, self-contained HTML report, not an embedded reference page. The report's recognizable structure and interactions come from this method; names, counts, dates, readings and operational policies come from this run's inputs. Read the manifest, published project configuration, context and interval CSV first. Preserve the selected reporting period.

## Analysis contract

- Sum eligible valid interval consumption under the published meter mapping, avoiding parent/submeter double counting. Derive centre and circuit counts, facility groups and all tables from current inputs. Retain recorded totals even where readings are suspicious.
- Reconcile raw Excel/CSV coverage with expected centre × circuit × interval keys; report missing, duplicates, quality exclusions and genuine zero values separately. A zero does not prove a switched-off circuit; an outlier does not prove a fault. Keep synthetic/demo provenance prominent whenever supplied.
- Resolve operating windows per centre and effective date, including exceptions and holidays from published configuration. Do not hardcode opening hours, national holidays or centre count. Off-hours consumption is not automatically waste; in-hours consumption is not necessarily productive.
- Use area and configured population only when present and positive. Population definition must be stated (for example staff + customers). Mark provisional metadata next to benchmark results. Compute monthly energy/area and energy/person; annualizing one calendar month by ×12 is only a labelled scenario, not an annual measurement. For other durations use a stated time-normalization rule or omit annualization.
- Compute P50/P75 with linear interpolation at (n−1)p on sorted values. Show sample counts and facility types. Within-type comparisons are primary; an all-portfolio quadrant is supplementary and must identify its cohort. Small or heterogeneous groups cannot establish industry efficiency. Above both P75 thresholds means review priority, not proven inefficiency or a causal diagnosis.
- For spikes compare the same circuit, hour and day type using valid complete comparable days; state baseline, sample count, relative threshold and absolute magnitude. If history is insufficient, label a descriptive candidate anomaly rather than inventing a historical baseline. Do not copy fixed anomaly sites, thresholds, scores or counts from a prior report. Excluding a candidate interval is only a sensitivity scenario that removes potentially legitimate energy, never corrected consumption.
- A shutdown/SOP compliance percentage requires an explicit policy, threshold and eligible denominator. Without those inputs use a clearly labelled after-hours screening table, not a fabricated compliance score.
- Cost uses only an applicable configured tariff and tax basis. Missing tariff: show "Not configured" and omit currency values. Forecasts require a declared model, comparable-day counts and uncertainty. With no following-month observations, never synthesize an actual-to-date series or an on-track status. Retain a short outlook panel explaining the missing evidence and next-period checks.

## Five-section information architecture

Preserve this order and navigation; show a compact unavailable state where a section lacks evidence rather than fabricating it. This is the user's explicitly selected reference layout: its overview order takes precedence over the shared presentation Skill's default findings-first ordering, while retaining that Skill's evidence and concise-writing requirements. At the top of Overview put the KPI band, then the facility-type table, then the linked key findings. Do not prepend a long findings stack or duplicate provenance notices. One synthetic badge in the header is sufficient; detailed data status belongs below the overview briefing.

1. **Overview.** Report title and explicit analysis dates, portfolio count and source status. A dark horizontal KPI band for centres, total energy and cost/status. Below it, a facility-type table (outlets, kWh, cost if supported, share), then 3–4 concise key findings linked to their detailed sections. Each finding combines a number, implication and next check. This is a portfolio briefing, not a grid of disconnected diagnostics.
2. **Benchmarks.** A highlighted priority recommendation derived from current benchmark calculations. A labelled EUI/per-person scatter quadrant with P75 crosshairs and facility-type colours, plus separate by-type EUI and per-person benchmark tables. Clicking/expanding a type row reveals its actual centre distribution and P50/P75 markers, sample count and centre values. An empirical histogram/dot distribution is preferable to implying a normal distribution from a tiny cohort. Provide centre detail on point selection and an accessible table alternative. Recompute all priorities; do not carry over reference centre letters.
3. **After-hours use.** Match the reference's chart-plus-ranking layout: appliance composition, kWh/share table, then centre/day/circuit spike candidates and a shutdown-policy or screening view. Switching between energy and share changes the displayed measure and unit. Selecting a centre or anomaly exposes its hourly evidence. Distinguish necessary base loads from unverified control opportunities.
4. **Operating hours.** Mirror the after-hours layout for direct comparison: appliance composition and ranking, weekday/closed-day average hourly profile, and operating-hour candidates. Show complete-day sample counts. Explain the strongest difference without duplicating the same recommendation across sections.
5. **Outlook.** Next-period forecast versus observed-to-date only when supported. Otherwise show an honest readiness/next-month verification panel in the same position. No invented future actuals, invented savings or claims of live updates. End with an actionable owner/next-check table and compact assumptions/provenance.

## Charles-style visual specification

Recreate the supplied reference's report composition: full-width navy header, navy sticky horizontal five-link navigation with light text and a blue active underline, generous light-grey canvas and broad white section panels. At desktop use a 1240px maximum content width with 24px gutters, 32px top spacing, 26px panel padding and 40–52px between sections. Use Segoe UI/system sans-serif; main title about 34px, section title 24px, body 15–16px. Palette: ink #1a1a2e, navy #0f3460, page #f0f2f5, white #ffffff, blue #2980b9, amber #e6a817. Benchmark accents may use purple; investigation accents use muted red. Tables align numeric columns right, with light header fills and thin row separators. Use a two-column chart/table split where readable, collapsing below 880px. One broad KPI band and connected sections, not a generic landing-page card wall.

Match the reference's hierarchy and useful interactions, not its hardcoded numbers, unsupported "phantom waste" wording, erroneous centre count or externally hosted chart dependency. Do not include application navigation, login, simulated chat or fake workflow buttons.

## Interaction and output contract

Implement offline inline SVG/HTML and a small inline JavaScript controller. No CDN, network calls, external fonts, iframe embedding or runtime dependency. Use real buttons/selects with labels and keyboard focus; expanded content must be keyboard-accessible, and dialogs support Escape/close/focus return. Keep chart data in one computed embedded JSON payload; filters, tables, tooltips and summary values must use that same payload. Do not fabricate independent data to make a control work.

Required working interactions: sticky section anchors; benchmark group expansion; centre selection with corresponding details; after-hours energy/share switch. A reset returns to all centres. Explicitly label a local filter's scope so it does not falsely imply that the official whole-period headline changed. Charts remain understandable without hover. Detailed tables may scroll inside their containers; the page itself must not overflow at 390px. Set min-width:0 on grid/flex children, use minmax(0,1fr) tracks, constrain SVG/select/table-wrapper widths, and collapse every split including anomaly/hourly detail layouts on mobile. Verify document scrollWidth equals viewport width after expanding groups and changing selections, not just on the initial screen. Do not merely hide overflow and clip evidence. Print styles expand necessary evidence and hide controls without hiding report content.

Benchmark disclosure summaries must wrap both the group name and the P50/P75 statistics on narrow screens. Do not apply nowrap to long statistics spans; use flex-wrap, min-width:0 and normal wrapping. A narrow container alone does not prevent its text from causing document overflow. Test closed and expanded summaries.

Save outputs/report.html, computed facts and a short method/coverage note. Verify aggregates, benchmark cohort sizes, section links and every implemented control. The system owns generation time; omit a generation timestamp unless explicitly supplied by system metadata. Keep report analysis dates separate. Review for both numeric integrity and reference alignment before declaring completion.
