# Evidence-led report improvements — 20 September 2026

## Product decision

Reports should help a reader decide what to change. Preserve free exploration and project style; select a primary evidence graphic for each decision rather than adding decorative charts. Homepage summaries should be a short decision-oriented sentence. Never manufacture higher savings or safety findings.

## Candidate methods

- energy-report-investigation 0.6.0: investigate controllable contributors, recurrence, comparable exposure and rejected larger opportunities; preserve reproducible chart data; distinguish measured spending from conditional removable load.
- report-presentation 1.5.0: match charts to questions (control-window profile, weekly heatmap, aligned comparison or confirmed intervention); label missing data, units and conditions; candidate summary at most 18 words.
- report-review 0.4.3: verify chart-to-calculation consistency, avoid misleading benefits and inspect candidate summary/value selection.

No generated HTML is manually edited. Published equipment names and reference spatial geometry remain authoritative. No forced chart quota or invented third opportunity. Changes apply through actual Skill inputs; editing a built-in analysis template does not replace an existing project's saved analysis Skill.
