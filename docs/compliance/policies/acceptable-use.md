# Acceptable Use Policy

**Owner:** People lead · **Approved by:** [CEO name] · **Effective:** [YYYY-MM-DD] · **Review:** yearly · **Maps to:** CC1.1, CC6.8

This policy applies to all [Company] staff and contractors.

## Devices

- **MDM.** Work devices are enrolled in MDM, with:
  - full-disk encryption
  - screen lock after 5 minutes or less
  - automatic updates
  - endpoint protection
- **No customer data on unmanaged or personal devices.**
- **Lost or stolen devices** must be reported within 24 hours.

## Accounts

- Use the company password manager, with a unique password for each account.
- Turn on MFA everywhere it is offered.
- Never share accounts or MFA devices.
- Never put secrets in code, tickets, chat, email or AI tools.

## Customer data

- Access customer data only when your job needs it. Use super-admin access only for support requests; it is logged.
- Do not copy production data to a laptop without the Engineering lead's approval. Any copy must be encrypted and deleted afterwards.
- Use only anonymised data for testing.

## AI tools

- Use only AI tools that are listed in the vendor register.
- Never paste customer data, credentials or code containing secrets into an unapproved tool.

## Communication

- Watch for phishing.
- Confirm any request for payment, credentials or access changes through a second channel.
- Do not auto-forward work email to personal accounts.

## Reporting

Report suspected incidents, lost devices or policy breaches immediately to [security email / channel]. Nobody is penalised for reporting in good faith.
